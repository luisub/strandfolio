# Data notices

The wheel and source distribution include Strandfolio’s original BSD-3-Clause software, the feature and ladder catalogues, and an annotated GFP example from Dryad. The source-specific terms below apply to those data. Each archive includes the Strandfolio license and the SB2CL MIT notice.

The bundled GFP plasmid and its catalogue fingerprints come from the Dryad deposit by Weeks and colleagues, released under CC0 1.0. Other feature catalogue entries derive from the Kipcak and Erisir reference (CC0 1.0), the T2T Consortium's CHM13v2.0 assembly (CC0 1.0), and the SB2CL library by Alejandro Vignoni and Yadira Boada (MIT). The SQLite catalogue records attribution per source. The distribution includes the SB2CL copyright notice and license. [Dryad](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.c59zw3rkb) · [Kipcak and Erisir](https://doi.org/10.18130/V3/NEGJWP) · [CHM13v2.0](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/genome/GCA_009914755.4/) · [SB2CL](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18828308)

The ladder catalogue includes derived lengths and identifiers from INSDC deposits and comparison data cited in the catalogue. Strandfolio adapted variant definitions and two nominal lengths from *The pPSU Plasmids for Generating DNA Molecular Weight Markers* by Ryan C. Henrici, Turner J. Pecen, James L. Johnston and Song Tan (2017), licensed under CC BY 4.0. The protein reference includes mature-chain intervals and calculated masses derived from UniProt Consortium data (CC BY 4.0) and wwPDB entry 1AVU (CC0 1.0).
